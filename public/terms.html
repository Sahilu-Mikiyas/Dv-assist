What we do

We help you prepare a U.S. Diversity Visa (DV) lottery entry: we check your details twice, prepare your photos and passport pictures, and, depending on your package, file the entry for you on the official site, pay the official entry fee and give you the confirmation number. The packages and prices are on our home page.

What we are not

  • We are an independent business, not the U.S. Government, and not endorsed by it.
  • Selection is random. Nobody, including us, can improve your chances, and we never promise that you will be selected.
  • Entries are only made on the official site, dvprogram.state.gov. Anyone can enter there themselves.

Your part

  • Give true, complete details, exactly as in your passport, including your spouse and every unmarried child under 21, even those who will not travel with you.
  • Enter only once. If you, or anyone for you, files another entry for you, the DV rules disqualify all your entries. Tell us if you have already entered this year.
  • Read your entry when we ask and give your approval. We file nothing without it.
  • Keep your resume code and your confirmation number safe. You need the confirmation number to check your result on the official site.

Our part

  • Two different people on our team check every entry before you approve it.
  • We file one entry per person, only after your approval, and give you the confirmation number.
  • We keep your data private and secure, as described in our privacy policy.

Payment and refunds

You pay the package price before we file your entry. Refunds follow our refund policy.

Limits of our responsibility

We are not responsible for decisions of the U.S. Government, changes to the DV rules or dates, the official site being unavailable, or the result of the lottery. If we make a mistake that stops your entry from being filed, our responsibility is limited to refunding what you paid us.

Referral partners

Partners who refer applicants agree to: never charge applicants anything extra; only refer people who asked for help; never promise anyone they will be selected; keep applicants' details private; and only share a phone number with that person's permission. Partners are paid as agreed in writing with us. We may suspend a partner who breaks these promises.

Changes, law and contact

We may update these terms and will post the new version here with its date. These terms are governed by the laws of Ethiopia. Contact: phone and office address to be added.
