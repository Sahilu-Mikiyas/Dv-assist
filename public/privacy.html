Who we are

Ethio DV Assist is an independent service in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, that helps people prepare and file entries for the U.S. Diversity Visa (DV) lottery. We are not part of, or endorsed by, the U.S. Government. We are responsible ("data controller") for the personal data described here under Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

What we collect

  • About you and your family: names, gender, date, city and country of birth, country of eligibility and residence, mailing address, phone, email, education, marital status, and the same birth details for your spouse and each child under 21.
  • Passport: passport number, issuing country, expiry date, and pictures of the photo page and, if separate, the signature page.
  • Photos of each person on the entry.
  • Payments: your package, the amount you paid, how (Telebirr, CBE Birr, bank or cash) and the transaction number; refunds.
  • Your entry's progress, and the confirmation number from the official site once filed.
  • Security records: which staff member did what and when, and the internet address (IP) of sign-ins and changes.
  • Referral partners: name, organisation, phone, city and the Telebirr number for payouts; and the name and phone of people they introduce, with those people's permission.

Your resume code is stored only in a scrambled form (a "hash"): we cannot read it back.

Why we use it

  • To prepare, check and file one DV entry per person on the official site, as you asked us to.
  • To contact you about your entry, your approval, your payment and your confirmation number.
  • To take payments and give refunds, and to pay referral partners their agreed share.
  • To prevent mistakes and fraud, including duplicate entries, which disqualify people.
  • To keep records the law requires.

We use your data because you ask us to provide this service and agree to these terms, and, for security and fraud records, because we have a legitimate need to keep the service safe.

Who can see it

  • Our checking team, once you send your entry for checking. Each person signs in with their own account and an authenticator code, and every action is recorded.
  • The partner who referred you sees only your name, package, how far your entry has gone and whether you have paid. Never your details, photos or passport.
  • The U.S. Department of State, when we file your entry on dvprogram.state.gov. Their privacy rules then apply to that copy.
  • Companies that run parts of our service for us, only for that purpose: our hosting (Render) and database (Turso), with servers in Frankfurt, Germany; and, when switched on, our SMS (AfroMessage) and email (Brevo) providers, which receive your phone number or email and the message. Our card provider (Bitnob) issues cards to us, not to you, and receives none of your personal data.
  • Fonts on our pages are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your device's internet address.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

How we protect it

Your details, photos and passport pictures are encrypted (AES-256) before they are stored, so a copy of the database alone reveals nothing. Staff accounts need a password and an authenticator code; partners sign in with a personal code. Messages that contain codes are erased once delivered.

How long we keep it

  • Entries that were filed: until we decide the period.
  • Entries never sent for checking, and invitations never used: until we decide the period.

When the time is up, your details, photos, passport pictures, codes and confirmation number are erased automatically. We keep only the entry number, its progress and the security records. People selected in the lottery need their details for the visa forms well into the following year, which is why filed entries are kept longer.

Your rights

  • See the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Have it corrected. Before your entry is filed, you can change it yourself or ask us; once filed, the official entry itself cannot be changed.
  • Have it erased. We erase it on request, except while a payment card for your entry is being used, and except records we must keep by law. An entry already filed on the official site cannot be withdrawn by us.
  • Withdraw your permission for things that rely on it, such as a partner sharing your number.
  • Complain to the Ethiopian Communications Authority, which supervises data protection.

To use these rights, contact us with your entry number (for example EDA-0012). We may ask for your resume code or passport to confirm it is you.

Children

The DV form requires every unmarried child under 21 to be listed. We collect children's details only from the parent or guardian who makes the entry, and only for that entry.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in (for your entry, a partner account or staff work). No tracking or advertising cookies.

Changes and contact

We will post any change here with a new date. Contact: phone and office address to be added.

Independent service. Not affiliated with the U.S. Government. Entries are made only on the official site, dvprogram.state.gov.
