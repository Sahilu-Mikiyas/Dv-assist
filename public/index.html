A photo that doesn't qualify

Wrong size, a shadowed background, glasses or an old photo. Each can get an entry disqualified. We check every photo before it goes in.

A family member left off

Every spouse and every unmarried child under 21 must be listed, even those who won't travel. We walk through your family with you, name by name.

More than one entry

Submitting twice, even by accident or through two agents, disqualifies you. We file exactly one entry and give you the proof.

Questions, answered honestly

ጥያቄዎች፣ በግልጽ የተመለሱ

Can you increase my chances of being selected?

No one can. Selection is random. What we do is make sure your entry is complete and correct, so it isn't disqualified for a mistake.

What does the official fee cover, and what do you charge?

The official fee is set by the U.S. Department of State. Our service fee is separate and shown before you pay. Nothing is hidden.

I'm married with children. Do they all need to be listed?

Yes. Your spouse and every unmarried child under 21 must be included, even if they won't travel with you. Leaving anyone out can disqualify the entry.

What happens to my personal data?

Your details and photos are encrypted and used only for your entry. Only our checking team can see them.
