Last updated 7 November 2026. The Amharic version is being prepared; until then the English text applies. · የአማርኛ ትርጉም በዝግጅት ላይ ነው።

These rules are a proposal for Ethio DV Assist to confirm. Prices are on our home page.

Full refund

  • You cancel before our team has started checking your entry.
  • We cannot file your entry because of a mistake on our side.
  • The entry period closes, or the U.S. Government cancels it, before we file your entry.

Partial refund

You cancel after our team has checked your entry but before it is filed: we refund the price minus 300 ETB for the checking work.

No refund

  • After your entry has been filed on the official site: it cannot be withdrawn, and the official fee has been paid.
  • For not being selected. Selection is random.
  • If you gave false or incomplete details, or entered more than once.

How refunds are paid

Ask us with your entry number. We pay refunds within 14 days, to the same Telebirr or CBE Birr account you paid from, or in cash at our office if you paid in cash. Every refund is recorded with its reason.

Independent service. Not affiliated with the U.S. Government. Entries are made only on the official site, dvprogram.state.gov.
