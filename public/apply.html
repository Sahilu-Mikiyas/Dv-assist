How to take a photo that passes · ትክክለኛ ፎቶ እንዴት እንደሚነሳ

  • Stand in front of a plain white wall, a step away from it
  • Face a window or daylight. No flash shadows
  • Remove glasses. Keep the head uncovered unless for religious reasons
  • Taken in the last 6 months, looking straight at the camera
