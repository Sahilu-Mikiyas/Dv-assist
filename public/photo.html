    The official rules

    • Exactly 600 × 600 pixels, JPEG, 240 KB or less
    • Head fills 50–69% of the height, centred
    • Plain white or off-white background, no shadows
    • In colour, in focus, taken in the last 6 months
    • No glasses, eyes open, facing the camera

    Good to know

    This check catches the most common problems, but it is not an official decision. When we prepare your entry, a person on our team checks every photo too.
